Featured Project

Borehole Project Duk County

Bringing clean water and hope to the heart of South Sudan.

Clean Water Initiative

Quenching Thirst, Building Futures

In the remote communities of Duk County, access to clean water has always been a challenge. Women and children walked miles daily, missing out on education and economic opportunities.

The Borehole Project was initiated as an inspection and assessment mission to understand the community's water needs. The findings revealed that the entire community depends on this single water point for their daily survival.

This one borehole serves over 2,000 people, but the demand far exceeds the supply. More boreholes and water infrastructure are urgently needed to ease the burden of dependence on a single source. Your support can help us drill more wells and bring sustainable water access to this community.
