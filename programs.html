Education for Every Child

Breaking the cycle of poverty through learning

We believe that education is the foundation of a prosperous society. Our education program provides access to quality learning for underprivileged children and youth across South Sudan.

  • School scholarships
  • School supplies & uniforms
  • Teacher training & support
  • Literacy & numeracy programs
  • Girls' education initiatives
  • After-school tutoring
5,000+ Children Educated
25 Schools Supported

Health & Wellness

Delivering essential healthcare to remote communities

Access to quality healthcare is a basic human right. Our health program delivers essential medical services, maternal care, and health education to remote and underserved communities across South Sudan.

  • Mobile health clinics
  • Maternal & child health
  • Vaccination campaigns
  • Health education workshops
  • Clean water initiatives
  • Mental health support

500+ Mothers Supported
