Our Story

Dedicated to Transforming Lives

The Deng Johnson Malual Leek Foundation was founded with a singular vision: to foster sustainable development and social progress in South Sudan. What began as a personal commitment to give back has grown into a comprehensive non-profit organization serving thousands of people across the country.

Our founder, Deng Johnson Malual Leek, witnessed firsthand the challenges facing his community — lack of access to education, healthcare, and economic opportunity. Determined to make a difference, he established the foundation to create lasting, meaningful change.

Today, we work tirelessly to empower communities through education, health initiatives, and community development programs. Every project we undertake is driven by a deep commitment to dignity, equity, and the belief that every person deserves a chance to thrive.

— Deng Johnson Malual Leek

Our Journey

A History of Impact

Milestones that have shaped our foundation.

2020

Foundation Established

The Deng Johnson Malual Leek Foundation was officially founded with a mission to serve communities across South Sudan.

2021

First Education Program Launched

Partnered with local schools to provide scholarships, school supplies, and teacher training to underprivileged communities.

2022

Health & Wellness Initiative

Expanded into healthcare, delivering essential medical services, maternal care, and health education to remote areas.

2023

Community Development Programs

Launched peace-building workshops, economic empowerment projects, and infrastructure support across 12 communities.

2024

Expansion & Partnership Growth

Partnered with international NGOs and local organizations to scale impact, reaching over 5,000 lives.

2025

Today — Building a Brighter Future

Continuing to expand our programs and deepen our impact, always guided by our mission to serve South Sudan.
